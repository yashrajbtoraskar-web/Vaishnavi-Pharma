FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Within Pune city limits, we deliver within 30–60 minutes. Suburban areas may take up to 2 hours.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, net banking, debit/credit cards, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

🔄 Can I return medicines?

Unopened medicines in original packaging can be returned within 7 days with valid reason. Prescription medicines cannot be returned.

📄 Do I need a prescription for all medicines?

Only medicines marked "Rx" require a prescription. OTC (over-the-counter) medicines can be ordered freely.
